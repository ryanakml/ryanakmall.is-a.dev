# Phase 01 — Identity Discovery

**Enter:** Fresh workspace initialized. **Exit:** user explicitly approves Checkpoint A.

## Purpose

Discover who the person is before determining what the website looks like. Engineer identity is relevant, but should not dictate developer-themed design.

## Agent ownership

**A — Identity & Narrative Architect (primary):** design a responsive interview and distill findings. The lead asks the user the questions and relays answers. Use small sets of roughly 3–4 questions, prioritizing questions that materially change positioning, narrative or site experience. Avoid data already provided during *this* discovery; do not retrieve past chats or local biography.

Learn progressively: motivations, professional goals, target visitors, work worth showcasing, values, personality, humor, voice, language, preferred personal boundaries, desired impression and call to action. Ask only for relevant biographical detail, not a complete autobiography.

**B — Visual Researcher (parallel when supported):** begin open-ended research on outstanding independent personal sites, art directors, editorial sites and creative studios. Use A1 Gallery MCP if connected; also explore Minimal Gallery and Siteinspire. Capture useful URLs and lessons, but do not decide the final visual direction. Stop at a compact first shortlist; deeper identity-fit evaluation belongs to Phase 02.

## Tool readiness

Verify Taste Skill is installed and its actual SKILL.md can be read. If not, check https://github.com/Leonxlnx/taste-skill for the current installation method; seek required permissions. Verify A1 MCP connection using https://www.a1.gallery/mcp. Authentication, global installs or permission changes require appropriate approval. A1 failure does not block the identity interview. Do not fabricate tool access.

## Outputs

Create concise documents as evidence accumulates:

- `outputs/identity-brief.md`: true background, positioning, audience, priorities, voice, content needs, privacy boundaries, possible design implications, outstanding questions.
- `state/facts.md`: distinguish *user-confirmed*, *publicly verified with permission*, *unverified*, and *do not publish* facts.
- `outputs/reference-shortlist.md`: preliminary links and observations, not a design commitment.

## Checkpoint A — Stop and ask

Present a readable synthesis of: who the user is, professional positioning, 2–3 visitor impressions, editorial voice, proposed content priorities, what to avoid, and material uncertainties. Ask for corrections and explicit approval. No finalized art direction, prototype build, or production code before approval. Upon approval, record the decision in `state/decisions.md` and only then load `phases/02-art-direction.md`.
