# Phase 02 — Research Synthesis & Art Direction

**Enter only after Checkpoint A approval.** **Exit:** two justified, meaningfully different art directions are ready for implementation. There is no extra approval gate here; go on to Phase 03.

## Roles

**B — Visual Researcher:** assess curated references against the approved identity. **C — Taste Art Director:** synthesizes aesthetic and editorial direction using installed Taste Skill. **D — Technical Architect:** evaluates the smallest stack capable of realizing both designs; do not import old portfolio code. Lead owns decisions and integration.

## Research contract

Primary structured source: https://www.a1.gallery/mcp, if connected. Use *actual available* search, section, design-token and font capabilities selectively. Curated sources: https://minimal.gallery/, https://www.siteinspire.com/, https://www.a1.gallery/. Examine roughly 10–15 distinct high-quality references, shortlist 5–7. For shortlisted examples record URL, visual evidence if available, typography, composition, grids, imagery, navigation/motion, what problem is solved, identity fit, and what **not** to copy. Skip unreachable sources honestly and use alternatives; do not browse indefinitely.

Typography: consult https://www.typewolf.com/ and A1 font resources where accessible. Compare at least three plausible systems (display, body, optional utility fonts). Check readability, mobile hierarchy, language support, performance and licensing. Do not assume a showcased font is freely usable.

Taste Skill is a primary method, not an aesthetic oracle. Read the installed instructions and use its relevant art-direction process. Identity fit beats flashy showcase effects.

## Art direction outputs

Create `outputs/art-directions.md` with **two genuinely different** proposed experiences. Explain for each:

- Core idea and why it belongs to this person.
- Editorial structure and actual content mapping.
- Typography, composition, grid, color and visual language.
- Photography/graphic strategy, navigation and motion philosophy.
- Treatment of engineering work without coder stereotypes.
- Mobile behavior, accessibility implications, tradeoffs and expected build complexity.
- Short, explicit reference-to-decision mapping; do not reproduce other sites.

Technical architect writes a brief, reasoned stack recommendation. Astro/Next.js/etc. are candidates, not preselected answers. Decide based on real creative and functional requirements.

Do not spend effort on a polished deck instead of implementing prototypes. Once both art directions are defensible, record the decision to proceed and load `phases/03-prototypes.md`.
